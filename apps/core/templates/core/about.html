ABOUT MEDIGUARD

Medical intelligence for connected healthcare.

MEDIGUARD is a healthcare intelligence, screening, referral and follow-up platform designed to support structured healthcare workflows and better-informed clinical decisions.

THE PLATFORM

From screening to follow-up.

MEDIGUARD connects important healthcare workflows into a structured platform. It helps healthcare teams collect information, identify risk, coordinate referrals, monitor antimicrobial resistance and maintain continuity of care.

RESPONSIBLE INTELLIGENCE

AI that assists healthcare professionals.

MEDIGUARD is designed around assistive intelligence. AI capabilities support qualified healthcare workers rather than replacing clinical judgment or making autonomous diagnoses and prescriptions.
