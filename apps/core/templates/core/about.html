ABOUT MEDIGUARD

Medical Intelligence for Connected Healthcare

MEDIGUARD is a digital healthcare platform designed to support structured screening, clinical decision support, antimicrobial resistance monitoring, referrals, facility coordination, follow-up care, analytics, notifications, and healthcare auditing.

What is MEDIGUARD?

MEDIGUARD stands for a Medical Intelligence, Screening, Referral and Follow-up Platform. It brings important healthcare workflows into one structured digital environment.

The platform helps healthcare workers organize patient information, conduct structured screening, review clinical information, monitor antimicrobial resistance, coordinate referrals, manage follow-up activities, and use analytics to understand healthcare operations.

MEDIGUARD Workflow

A connected workflow from early detection to continuous learning.

01. Detect

Capture structured patient information and identify important health concerns through screening.

02. Assess

Organize screening findings and support healthcare workers in reviewing patient risk information.

03. Protect

Support antimicrobial resistance monitoring and responsible clinical follow-up.

04. Connect

Coordinate referrals between patients, healthcare workers, and healthcare facilities.

05. Follow Up

Track scheduled follow-ups and maintain continuity of care after screening or referral.

06. Learn

Use analytics, notifications, and audit information to improve healthcare workflows and accountability.

Core Capabilities

One platform connecting multiple healthcare workflows.

Patient Management

Maintain structured patient profiles and longitudinal healthcare information.

Health Screening

Conduct structured screening and maintain screening responses and assessments.

Clinical Support

Provide healthcare workers with structured information to support clinical review.

AMR Monitoring

Record antimicrobial resistance information, organisms, antibiotics, and resistance tests.

Referral Coordination

Create, track, and manage referrals between healthcare facilities.

Follow-up Care

Schedule and track follow-up activities to support continuity of care.

Data & Operational Intelligence

MEDIGUARD provides analytics, notifications, facility information, and audit trails to help healthcare teams understand activities and maintain operational accountability.

  • Healthcare analytics
  • Facility coordination
  • Notifications and follow-up reminders
  • Auditable healthcare activities

Responsible AI

MEDIGUARD is designed so that artificial intelligence supports qualified healthcare workers rather than replacing clinical judgment.

AI-assisted outputs are intended to support assessment and review. Clinical decisions, diagnosis, treatment, and prescriptions remain the responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals.

Detect. Assess. Protect. Connect. Follow Up. Learn.

MEDIGUARD brings these workflows together to create a more structured, connected, and accountable digital healthcare environment.
